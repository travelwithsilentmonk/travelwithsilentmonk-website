About & Contact

Last updated: 23 October 2026

TravelWithSilentMonk is an independent travel information site and YouTube channel. It has guides for travellers worldwide, with places to visit, festivals, food, stays, practical and visa information, and 5, 7 and 10-day itineraries.

What this site is

The guides are written in our own words as general information. Prices, visa rules and opening details change often, so always confirm with official sources before you travel. Where we show a "checked" date, that is when we last reviewed the information.

How the site earns money

Some links to booking and travel services are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you book, at no extra cost to you. Read our Affiliate Disclosure for the details.
