FESTIVAL GUIDE • NAGALAND, INDIA

Hornbill Festival

The "Festival of Festivals" — ten days where all of Nagaland's Naga tribes gather at Kisama to share music, food, dance and centuries of tradition.

DATES 2026 1 – 10 December
VENUE Kisama Heritage Village
NEAREST CITY Kohima (~12 km)
TRIP STATUS 📍 SilentMonk visits Dec 2026

OVERVIEW

What is the Hornbill Festival?

Started in 2000 by the Nagaland government, the Hornbill Festival brings all major Naga tribes together at Kisama Heritage Village, each hosting their own traditional morung. Expect war dances, folk songs, indigenous games, a craft bazaar, tribal food stalls and an evening rock music contest — all set against Nagaland's green hills. It's named after the hornbill bird, a symbol of courage in Naga folklore.

BACKPACKER ESSENTIALS

How to Get There

Nagaland's remoteness is the biggest question backpackers have — here's the straightforward route.

✈️ By Air

Fly into Dimapur Airport, Nagaland's only airport, with connections from Kolkata, Delhi and Guwahati. From Dimapur, it's roughly a 3-hour shared taxi or bus ride to Kohima.

🚆 By Train

Dimapur is also the nearest railhead, well connected to major Indian cities. Budget travelers often combine train + shared sumo (shared taxi) to keep costs low.

🚕 Kohima to Kisama

The festival ground at Kisama is about 12 km from Kohima. Shared taxis run regularly during festival days, or stay closer at Kigwema village, right next to the venue.

What Should You Budget?

Backpacker-tier Hornbill trips (shared transport, budget guesthouses, festival entry) typically start around ₹20,000–25,000 for a 4–5 day trip, excluding flights to Dimapur.
